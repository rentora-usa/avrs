Server rules

Ten short rules. By joining the server you agree to follow all of them.

1

Rule 1: Be respectful

Treat everyone decently. No harassment, hate speech, slurs, threats, or targeted bullying, in chat, signs, builds, usernames, or skins.

2

Rule 2: No griefing or stealing

Don't break, burn, blow up, or change anything that belongs to someone else, and don't take items from their chests or bases. "It's a random server" is not an excuse.

3

Rule 3: No cheating

No hacked clients, x-ray, fly, kill aura, auto-clickers, or anything else that gives an unfair advantage. If you're not sure whether a mod is okay, ask staff before you use it.

4

Rule 4: No exploits or duping

Don't use bugs to duplicate items or break the game. If you find one, report it to staff instead.

5

Rule 5: PvP needs consent

Only fight players who agreed to fight. Killing someone who didn't opt in, or trapping them, counts as griefing.

6

Rule 6: No spam or advertising

Don't flood chat, and don't advertise other servers, channels, or products. Never share someone else's personal information.

7

Rule 7: Don't lag the server

Giant redstone clocks, runaway mob farms, entity cramming, and other lag machines make the server unplayable for everyone. Staff may remove them.

8

Rule 8: Keep it appropriate

No sexual or graphic content in builds, signs, books, chat, or skins. Keep it suitable for all ages.

9

Rule 9: No ban evasion

Joining on another account to dodge a ban or mute gets both accounts banned and counts against any appeal.

10

Rule 10: Staff have the final say

Follow staff instructions. If you disagree with a decision, don't argue in chat. Use the appeals page.

What happens if you break a rule

Staff usually escalate in this order, but serious offenses like cheating, hate speech, or large-scale griefing can skip straight to a ban.

  1. Warning

    A heads-up so you can fix it.

  2. Mute or kick

    A short break from chat or the server.

  3. Temporary ban

    Locked out for a set amount of time.

  4. Permanent ban

    Removed from the server for good.

Think a punishment was a mistake? Send an appeal.
