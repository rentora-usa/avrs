Appeals

Think a ban or mute was a mistake, or ready to show you've changed? Tell staff what happened.

Before you appeal

  • Read the rules and work out which one the punishment is about.
  • Tell the truth. Staff can check logs, and a made-up story usually ends the appeal.
  • Appeal once. Sending the same appeal again won't speed anything up.
  • Don't join on another account while banned. That's ban evasion (rule 9) and counts against you.
  • Mutes and kicks can be appealed too.

What happens next

Staff review appeals as time allows and answer using the contact you provide, so make sure it's one you check.
